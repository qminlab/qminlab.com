QL-005/PRODUCTIVITY TOOL●IN USE

Claude Wait Board

An iPad status board that shows every running Claude task at a glance

Like a pickup board at a fast-food counter, each task gets a number and moves between Working, Needs attention and Done. Tasks waiting on a permission prompt flash the whole screen red, finished ones flash green, with a chime for each. A small server on the Mac collects live signals from Claude app chats and Claude Code.

macOS (server) iPad (board screen)
